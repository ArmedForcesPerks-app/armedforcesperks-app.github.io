What makes it different

  • Free for everyone. No paywall, no premium tier, and no ads.
  • Filter by your status. See which deals cover active duty, Guard and Reserve, veterans, retirees, or family members.
  • Clear "how to claim" steps. Each deal says where it works (online, in store, or both) and how eligibility is checked, such as ID.me, SheerID, or showing a military ID.
  • Checked against the source. Deals link to the business's own page and show when they were last confirmed.
  • Family and deployment perks. Not just shopping discounts, but also service pauses, fee waivers, and support programs.
  • Private by design. No account needed. Your saved deals stay on your phone, and the app does not track you. We never ask for your DoD ID number, Social Security number, or service records.

How we keep it free

Some deals in the app use affiliate links. If you buy something after tapping one, the business pays us a small commission. You are never charged anything extra because of it, every affiliate deal is labeled "Paid link" in the app, and whether a deal earns us anything never decides whether it is listed. Many deals we list earn us nothing at all. The deal links on our Deals page go straight to each business. Read our full affiliate disclosure.

Want to help keep it running?

Armed Forces Perks is free and always will be. Tips help cover the costs of running it, like app store fees, hosting and keeping deals up to date. Tipping is optional and never unlocks anything. Armed Forces Perks LLC is not a nonprofit, and tips are not tax-deductible.

Common questions

Is this an official military or government app?

No. Armed Forces Perks is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Defense, the VA, any branch of service, or any government agency.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. The deals you save stay on your phone.

Do I have to prove my service in the app?

No. We do not verify your status and do not collect military documents. Each business checks eligibility on its own, usually through a service like ID.me or SheerID, or by looking at your ID in store.

Are the deals guaranteed?

No. Businesses can change or end offers at any time. We check deals against each business's own information and show when they were last confirmed, but always confirm the details with the business before you buy.

When can I download it?

The Android app is on its way to Google Play. Until then, you can browse every deal in it on our Deals page. Email us if you would like to help test the app early.

Own a business with a military discount?

Tell us about it and we will review it for the app. Listing is free. Email armedforcesperks@gmail.com with your business name, the offer, who qualifies, how to claim it, and a link to where the offer is published.
