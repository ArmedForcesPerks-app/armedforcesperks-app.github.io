Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version. The Armed Forces Perks app does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your saved deals, savings tracker entries, searches, and settings stay on your phone and are never sent to us. To keep deals current, the app downloads our public deal list from our website, and that download includes nothing about you. If you use the Near me map, the app uses your approximate location only on your phone to show nearby stores. It is never saved and never sent to us or anyone else. We never ask for your DoD ID number, Social Security number, military documents, or payment details. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and to improve the app.

1. Who we are

Armed Forces Perks ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent app and website that helps members of the military community find discounts and benefits. Our developer name on Google Play is Armed Forces Perks. This policy explains how we handle information in the Armed Forces Perks mobile app (the "App") and at armedforcesperks.com (the "Website"), together the "Service." Armed Forces Perks is not a government service and is not affiliated with the Department of Defense or any branch of the military. You can reach us about privacy at armedforcesperks@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your phone

The App keeps some information on your phone so it works the way you set it up:

  • The deals you save.
  • Your savings tracker entries: the amounts, dates, and notes you type in to keep track of what you have saved.
  • Your settings, such as your filters (for example, the service status you choose, like "veteran" or "military spouse," and whether to show only confirmed deals) and light or dark mode.

This information stays on your phone. The App does not send it to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. The status you choose is only used to filter deals on your phone. It is never verified and never used to decide whether you qualify for anything.

The Near me map and your location. The Near me tab can show stores with a military discount near you. It asks for your location only when you tap Use my location, and you can browse the map without it. The App asks Android only for approximate location (never your precise location), only while you are using the App (never in the background). The App rounds your location to about 1 km (two-thirds of a mile), uses it on your phone to sort nearby stores and draw your position on the map, and then forgets it. Your location is not saved and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn location off for the App at any time in your phone's Settings.

Search runs entirely on your phone. What you type in the search box is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The savings tracker is only for your own records. Because your entries never leave your phone, we cannot see them, and we cannot recover them for you if you clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or lose your phone.

You can erase this information at any time. In the App, go to About and tap Clear saved data on this phone to erase your saved deals, savings tracker entries, filter settings, and the Near me map's saved store list and map images, or uninstall the App to erase everything. If your phone backs up app data to your Google Account, Android's own backup feature may include this information in your personal backup, which Google manages under its own privacy policy. We have no access to it.

3. Information we receive

From the App: nothing about you

The current version of the App does not have accounts and does not send any information about you to us or to any analytics, advertising, or tracking company. The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or push notification tools, and it does not access or use your phone's advertising ID.

Deal list updates. The App comes with a built-in deal list. To keep deals current, it also downloads the latest public deal list from our website (armedforcesperks.com). That download does not include any account, identifier, cookie, or other information about you. Like any visit to a website, it does reveal your device's IP address and basic technical details (such as the time of the request) to our website host, GitHub, which logs IP addresses for security purposes (see section 12). We do not use this to identify, track, or locate you.

Near me map

When you open the Near me tab, the App does two things over the internet. Neither one sends your location.

  • Store list. The App downloads one public list of store locations for the whole country from our website, the same file for everyone, and works out which stores are near you on your phone. Like the deal list download, it reveals your device's IP address to our website host, GitHub (see section 12), but nothing about where you are. The App checks for a new list at most about once a week.
  • Map images. Like any online map, the App downloads map images for the area shown on your screen from OpenFreeMap, a free map service. These requests include the map area being shown (which may be near you, because the map starts on your area) and, like any internet request, your device's IP address. They do not include your location reading, an account, an identifier, or cookies. OpenFreeMap says it does not use cookies or tracking, does not store IP addresses in its access logs, keeps error logs that may include IP addresses for up to 7 days, and may use Cloudflare to deliver map images. See OpenFreeMap's privacy policy.

Directions. If you tap Directions, the App opens your own maps app (such as Google Maps) with the store's address. The App adds nothing about you. Your maps app then works under its own privacy policy.

Store locations come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors. We build the list on GitHub's servers once a month; it contains only business locations, no information about users.

Emails you send us

The App's options to report a problem, suggest a deal, or send feedback open your own email app with a message already started. That message includes the deal's name and ID, or the App's version number for feedback. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it from your email app. If you send it, or email us directly, we receive your email address, your name if your email account shows it, your message, and anything else you choose to include.

Statistics from Google Play

Like all apps on Google Play, we can see statistics that Google provides to app developers, such as how many times the App has been installed, crash and performance reports from people who have chosen to share usage and diagnostic data with Google, and the public ratings and reviews people post. Crash and performance reports reach us without your name or contact information. Google collects this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect:

  • Your DoD ID number, Social Security number, military ID card, DD-214, orders, or any service records
  • Your rank, unit, duty station, or deployment details
  • Payment card or bank information (the App is free and has no purchases)
  • Your location (the Near me map uses your approximate location on your phone only, as described in section 2), and your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
  • Your advertising ID or any other device identifier
  • Your savings tracker entries or what you search for (these stay on your phone)
  • Health information

Military verification happens outside the App. When a business checks your eligibility through a service such as ID.me, SheerID, or VetRewards, or by looking at your ID in person, that happens directly between you and that business or service under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your verification documents or results.

5. How we use information

We use emails you send us only to reply to you, to fix, add, or update deals, and to improve the Service. We use Google Play statistics only to find and fix problems and to understand how many people use the App. We do not use any information for advertising, we do not build profiles about you, and we do not sell your information.

6. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Companies that run parts of the Service. Google provides our email account (Gmail), which holds the emails you send us, and distributes the App through Google Play. GitHub hosts this Website and the public deal and store lists the App downloads. OpenFreeMap provides the map images on the Near me map (it receives the map area on screen and your IP address, not your location; see section 3). Cloudflare forwards visitors from our other web address, armedforcesperks.org, to this Website. These companies handle information under their own privacy and security terms.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe in good faith it is needed to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfer. If Armed Forces Perks is ever transferred to a new owner, the emails we hold may be transferred as part of that, and they would stay covered by the promises in this policy.
  • With your permission. In any other case, only if you ask us to or agree to it.

7. Deal links, affiliate links, and other websites

When you tap a deal's link, the business's website opens in your web browser (sometimes shown as a browser tab on top of the App). From that point you are on the business's site, and its privacy policy applies, not ours. Some deals may use affiliate links. When you tap an affiliate link, the affiliate network or the business may use cookies or similar technology on its own website to record that you came from Armed Forces Perks, so it can credit us if you make a purchase. We do not control that technology. Affiliate networks generally report sales to us as totals and do not give us your name, contact details, or payment information.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use the App without giving us anything. No account or personal information is needed.
  • Erase what is on your phone with About > Clear saved data on this phone, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the emails you have sent us by emailing us. Deletion steps are on our Your data page.

We honor these requests for all users, wherever they live. Depending on your state (for example, California, Virginia, Colorado, and others), you may have additional rights under state privacy law, including the right to know what personal information we have, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. Because we do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, there is nothing to opt out of, but you may still contact us with any request. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. We will respond within 45 days, and we may need to confirm your identity first, usually by having you write from the same email address you used before. You may use an authorized agent where state law allows.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. We do not track you across other websites or apps, so these signals do not change how the Service works.

10. Security

Because the App keeps your information on your phone and does not send it to us, there is very little information about you in our hands. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the App and see your saved deals and savings tracker, so we recommend using a screen lock. The only personal information we hold is the email you choose to send us. We protect it, and the accounts we use to run the Service (our email, website hosting, and Google Play developer accounts), with strong passwords and two-step verification, and only the people who run Armed Forces Perks can access them. This Website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

11. Children

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Users under 18 should use the Service only with a parent or guardian's permission.

12. This website

The Website is a simple informational site. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts, and it has no forms. Our website host, GitHub Pages, logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's documentation and GitHub's privacy statement. This includes the App's downloads of the public deal and store lists. GitHub keeps these logs under its own policies, and we do not use them to identify or track anyone. If you visit our other address, armedforcesperks.org, Cloudflare forwards you here and may log your IP address or use security cookies under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

13. Where information is stored

The Service is operated from, and intended for users in, the United States. The emails you send us are stored by our email provider, which may store and process information in the United States and other countries. If you use the Service from outside the United States, including while stationed overseas, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

14. New features

If we add features that would collect information, for example optional accounts, we will update this policy and the App's Google Play data safety information before releasing those features.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top. If we make a significant change to how we collect or use personal information, we will tell you in the App (for example, in the update that brings the change) before the change takes effect.

16. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: armedforcesperks@gmail.com. Please do not email sensitive information such as your Social Security number, DoD ID number, or copies of military documents.
