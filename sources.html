Official sources

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Armed Forces Perks is not a government app and does not represent any government agency. It is independent and not affiliated with the VA, the DoD or any branch of the military. Information about government and military programs in the app and on this website comes from the official sources below. Each listing links to its source, and the agency decides who qualifies, so check the source for the full, current rules.

Where most of it comes from

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs: va.gov
  • Military OneSource (Department of Defense): militaryonesource.mil
  • TRICARE: tricare.mil
  • The military services and their programs: their official .mil sites, the Exchanges and commissaries (for example aafes.com and commissaries.com), and military colleges (for example usncc.edu and airuniversity.af.edu)
  • Other federal agencies such as the National Park Service (nps.gov) and the U.S. Department of Justice (justice.gov), and the U.S. Code (uscode.house.gov)
  • State programs: each state's own official site, usually a .gov address

VA claims and disability ratings

We do not help with VA claims or disability ratings, and we take no responsibility for them. We only share public information and point you to official sources. Filing a VA claim is free. For free help, go to VA.gov or a VA-accredited representative, such as a Veterans Service Organization. Check whether someone is accredited with VA's accreditation search, and report scams to VSAFE (833-38V-SAFE).
