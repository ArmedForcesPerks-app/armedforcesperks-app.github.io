Your data and how to delete it

For the Armed Forces Perks app by the developer Armed Forces Perks on Google Play

There is no account to delete. The current version of Armed Forces Perks does not have user accounts, and the app does not send any information about you to us. It only downloads our public deal list. Your saved deals, savings tracker entries, searches, and settings stay on your phone. Below is how to delete each kind of data that does exist.

1. Saved deals, savings tracker, and settings on your phone

This is everything the app stores: the deals you saved, your savings tracker entries (the amounts, dates, and notes you typed), and your settings, such as filters and light or dark mode. Search also runs on your phone, and nothing you type is sent to us. To erase your saved deals, savings tracker entries, and filter settings:

  1. Open the Armed Forces Perks app and go to About.
  2. Tap Clear saved data on this phone.

To erase everything, uninstall the app, or go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Armed Forces Perks > Storage > Clear storage (the wording varies by phone). Once erased, savings tracker entries cannot be restored by us, because we never had them. If your phone backs up app data to your Google Account, an older copy may remain in that personal backup until Android replaces it. Google manages that backup, and we cannot see it.

2. Emails and deal tips you sent us

If you emailed us, including through the app's report a problem, suggest a deal, or feedback options, we have your email address and message. To have them deleted:

  1. Email armedforcesperks@gmail.com from the same address you used.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my data.
  3. We will delete your messages within 30 days and reply to confirm. We keep only that request and our confirmation, for up to 2 years, as a record that we completed it.

Even if you do not ask, we delete emails after 2 years. If we already published a deal based on your tip, the deal stays in the app, but it never includes your name or contact information.

3. Data we do not have

We do not have accounts, profiles, savings tracker entries, searches, location history, device identifiers, or advertising data about you, so there is nothing else for us to delete. Statistics and reviews on Google Play are held by Google. You can delete a review you posted from the Google Play Store app. Our website host, GitHub, keeps its own security logs of visits to our website, including the app's deal list downloads, under GitHub's privacy statement.

4. If we add accounts later

If a future version of the app offers optional accounts, we will update this page with steps to delete your account and its data, both in the app and by email, before that version is released.

Questions? Email armedforcesperks@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
