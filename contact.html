What to write about

  • A deal is wrong or expired. Tell us the business name and what has changed. A link to the business's current page helps a lot.
  • Suggest a deal. Send the business name, the offer, who qualifies (active duty, Guard and Reserve, veterans, retirees, spouses, or family), how to claim it, and a link to where it is published.
  • Business owners. Want your military discount listed or updated? Listing is free. Send the same details as above.
  • App help or feedback. Tell us your phone model and what happened.
  • Privacy and your data. See our Privacy Policy and how to delete your data.
  • Security issues. If you find a security problem, please email us with the subject "Security" and give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing it publicly.

Please do not send sensitive information. Never email us your Social Security number, DoD ID number, military ID card, DD-214, orders, or other official documents. We will never ask for them.

Armed Forces Perks is not a government service. For questions about your military or VA benefits, contact your installation's family support, finance, or legal office, or the VA.
