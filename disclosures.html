Disclosures

Last updated: November 27, 2026

We want you to know exactly how Armed Forces Perks works. This page covers affiliate links, our independence from the government, VA claims and ratings, and the limits of the deal information we provide.

Affiliate disclosure

Armed Forces Perks is operated by Armed Forces Perks LLC. The app is free and has no banner or video ads. To help cover costs, some deals in the app use affiliate links. That means if you tap one of those links and then buy something or sign up for something, we earn a commission from the business or from an affiliate network that works with the business, such as CJ Affiliate, Impact or Rakuten Advertising.

  • You are never charged anything extra. Any commission is paid by the business, not added to your price. You pay exactly what you would pay without our link.
  • We label it where you see it. Every deal that uses an affiliate link is labeled "Paid link" in the app's deal list. Right above the button that opens the link, the app says "Paid link: we earn a commission if you buy. You pay nothing extra."
  • No affiliate links on this website. The deal links on our Deals page go straight to each business's own page.
  • Commissions do not decide what we list. We list deals because they are useful to the military community. Whether a deal earns us money does not affect whether it is listed or where it appears. Many deals we list earn us nothing at all.
  • No affiliate links for money or insurance offers. We do not use affiliate links for banks, credit unions, lenders, credit cards, investment companies, or insurance companies.
  • We do not get your payment details. Purchases happen on the business's own website or in its store. Affiliate networks report each qualifying sale to us (for example, the date, order amount, and our commission), without your name, contact details, or card information. See our Privacy Policy for how affiliate tracking works.

This disclosure is provided in keeping with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).

Tips

Armed Forces Perks LLC accepts optional tips through Ko-fi on this website, not in the app. Tips go to Armed Forces Perks LLC and help cover the costs of running the app, like app store fees, hosting and keeping deals up to date. Tipping is optional, never unlocks anything, and never affects which deals we list. Armed Forces Perks LLC is not a nonprofit, so tips are not tax-deductible.

VA claims and disability ratings

We do not help with VA claims or disability ratings, and we take no responsibility for them. Armed Forces Perks LLC and the people who run it are not accredited by the VA. We do not prepare, file, review, or give advice about VA claims, appeals, or ratings. The app and this website only show public information and point you to official sources. Only VA decides VA benefits and ratings.

We never list, recommend, or take money (including affiliate commissions) from companies that charge to help with VA claims or ratings.

Filing a VA claim is free. For free help, go to VA.gov or a VA-accredited representative, such as a Veterans Service Organization. Check whether someone is accredited with VA's accreditation search. If someone pressures you to pay for help with a claim, report it to VSAFE (833-38V-SAFE).

Business names and trademarks

Business names, product names, and trademarks shown in the app and on this website belong to their owners. We use them only to identify the business offering a deal. Listing a business does not mean it endorses Armed Forces Perks or has a partnership with us, unless we say so.

Where our information comes from, and its limits

Every listing in the app and on our Deals page links to its source so you can check it yourself. Business deals come from each business's own published information. For government and military programs, the source is the program's official website, usually a .gov or .mil address (or the program's own official site, like the Exchange or a military college). Our Official sources page lists every one. We show when each listing was last confirmed. Even so, businesses can change, limit, or end offers at any time without telling us, and terms can vary by location. The business decides who is eligible and how eligibility is checked. Please confirm the details with the business before you buy, and let us know if something is out of date so we can fix it.

Not professional advice

Information in the app and on this website is general information about consumer offers and programs. It is not legal, financial, tax, medical, or official benefits advice. For questions about your military or VA benefits, contact your installation's legal, finance, or family support office, the VA, or a VA-accredited Veterans Service Organization.
