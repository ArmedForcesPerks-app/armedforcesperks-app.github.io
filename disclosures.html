Disclosures

Last updated: November 24, 2026

We want you to know exactly how Armed Forces Perks works. This page covers affiliate links, our independence from the government, and the limits of the deal information we provide.

Affiliate disclosure

Armed Forces Perks is free and has no ads. To help cover costs, some deals may use affiliate links. That means if you tap one of those links and then buy something or sign up for something, we may earn a commission from the business or from an affiliate network that works with the business.

  • You do not pay more. Any commission is paid by the business, not added to your price.
  • We label it. Every deal that uses an affiliate link is labeled in the app.
  • Commissions do not decide what we list. We list deals because they are useful to the military community. Whether a deal earns us money does not affect whether it is listed or where it appears. Many deals in the app earn us nothing at all.
  • No affiliate links for money or insurance offers. We do not use affiliate links for banks, credit unions, lenders, credit cards, investment companies, or insurance companies.
  • We do not get your payment details. Purchases happen on the business's own website or in its store. Affiliate networks generally report sales to us as totals, without your name or card information.

This disclosure is provided in keeping with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).

Armed Forces Perks is an independent, privately operated app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to the U.S. Department of Defense (also called the Department of War), the Department of Veterans Affairs, the Department of Homeland Security, the U.S. Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard, National Guard, or any other government agency or military organization.

Names of military branches are used only to describe who a deal is for. We do not use official seals, emblems, insignia, or logos, and nothing in the app or on this site should be read as an official statement or endorsement by any government body or by any service member acting in an official capacity. The views expressed are our own.

Business names and trademarks

Business names, product names, and trademarks shown in the app belong to their owners. We use them only to identify the business offering a deal. Listing a business does not mean it endorses Armed Forces Perks or has a partnership with us, unless we say so.

Where our information comes from, and its limits

Every listing in the app links to its source so you can check it yourself. Business deals come from each business's own published information. For government and military programs, the source is the program's official website, usually a .gov or .mil address. We show when each listing was last confirmed. Even so, businesses can change, limit, or end offers at any time without telling us, and terms can vary by location. The business decides who is eligible and how eligibility is checked. Please confirm the details with the business before you buy, and let us know if something is out of date so we can fix it.

Map and store locations

The app's Near me map shows stores of businesses that have a military discount in the app. Store locations come from OpenStreetMap, a free map anyone can edit, and are © OpenStreetMap contributors. Our store list is available under the Open Database License. Map images are provided by OpenFreeMap (© OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors). Some stores may be missing, closed, or in the wrong place, and some businesses offer their discount only at participating locations. Please check with the store before you go. You can correct a store's location on OpenStreetMap, and the app will pick up the fix within about a month.

Not professional advice

Information in the app is general information about consumer offers and programs. It is not legal, financial, tax, or official benefits advice. For questions about your military or VA benefits, contact your installation's legal, finance, or family support office, or the VA.
